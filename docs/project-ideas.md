# Project Ideas

Candidate project ideas for the Houston AITX Community Hackathon, organized by track. See [hackathon-info.md](hackathon-info.md) for full track descriptions and judging criteria.

## Agents Track

> Build an AI agent capable of independent, meaningful action — real memory, tool use, and reasoning about what to do next, not an LLM API wrapper with a chat window. Strongest submissions push into multi-agent systems: dedicated roles, shared memory state, and long-horizon planning executing a complex workflow end to end.

- **Research agent** — takes a company name, scrapes the web, monitors competitors, and maintains a living brief it updates on its own schedule.
- **Multi-agent dev team** — one agent writes code, one reviews it, one runs tests, all sharing state until the feature ships.
- **Ops agent** — watches your inbox and calendar, drafts responses, books follow-ups, and escalates only what actually needs you.
- **Procurement agent** — compares vendor quotes, negotiates via email, and builds a recommendation with receipts.

## Houston Open Data Track

> Build with the city's own data (data.houstontx.gov) — turn it into a business insight, civic tool, or social impact application. Winning means surfacing something non-obvious that someone could act on tomorrow.

- **Site selection tool** — combines permit data, 311 complaints, and crime stats to score neighborhoods for small businesses.
- **Flood risk assistant** — merges drainage and elevation datasets with parcel data so homebuyers can check an address before they bid.
- **City services chatbot** — answers "when is my trash pickup" or "why is my street closed" straight from live city datasets.
- **Restaurant safety dashboard** — tracks health inspections against 311 food complaints to spot problem spots before they trend.

## Most Commercializable Track

> Build something with a clear path to a real business — a defined customer, a reason to pay, and a working product you could demo to an investor Monday morning.

- **Vertical SaaS tool** — automates a painful workflow for one specific industry, like permit tracking for contractors or intake triage for law firms.
- **Usage-based AI service** — upload your data, get the deliverable, pay per run.
- **Consumer app** — a working payment flow and a wedge feature people would actually pay $10/month for.
- **B2B integration** — plugs into tools companies already use (Slack, HubSpot, QuickBooks) and saves a measurable number of hours per week.
